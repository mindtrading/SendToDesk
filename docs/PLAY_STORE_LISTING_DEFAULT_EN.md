## Full description (4,000 max) — 3,332 / 4,000

```
Passport size photo maker and doc scanner that sends the file straight to your PC.

Take a passport photo or scan a document on your phone. SendToDesk removes the background, converts the scan to PDF, compresses it to the KB size you need, and delivers the file to your Windows or Mac computer over Wi‑Fi. No WhatsApp hop, no USB cable, no cloud upload.

Made for photo studios, Xerox and café counters, and offices that scan all day — and for anyone who needs a passport photo under 50 KB or a PDF under 200 KB for an online form.


PASSPORT SIZE PHOTO MAKER

• Camera guide for face position and lighting
• Background removed on the phone, then pick white, light blue, red or any custom colour
• Change passport photo background without a website
• Sizes: 35x45 mm ICAO (India, UK, EU), 2x2 inch (US, Philippines), 40x50 mm ID, 50x50 mm visa, or custom mm
• HD 300 DPI, print ready
• Save the passport photo under 100 KB, 50 KB or 30 KB for online applications
• Crop, move, brush and eraser for a clean edge
• One tap: Save & Send to Desktop

An ID photo and passport photo maker with the print file already on your computer.


DOC SCANNER — SCAN TO PDF

• Camera scanner with automatic edge detection, crop and cleanup
• Photo to PDF: import pictures from the gallery and convert them to a PDF
• Multi-page document scanner, save as PDF or JPG
• Compress PDF: High quality, Balanced, Small size, or type an exact KB limit
• One tap Save & Send to Desktop — the PDF lands on the PC, ready to print or upload

Scan and send documents in one flow instead of scanning in one app and sending in another.


COMPRESS PDF AND PHOTOS TO KB

Need a PDF under 100 KB, 200 KB, 300 KB or 500 KB for a government form, college upload or job portal? Pick the limit and SendToDesk rebuilds the scanned PDF to that size — 1 MB, 2 MB or any KB value you type. Passport photos can be compressed under 50 KB or 100 KB. Reduce PDF size on the phone, then send the small file to the PC or upload it directly.


SEND TO PC — PAIR ONCE, SEND ALL DAY

1. Install SendToDesk on Android and open SendToDesk Desktop on your Windows or Mac PC.
2. Put the phone and computer on the same Wi‑Fi.
3. Connect from Nearby devices, or scan the QR / enter the 6‑digit code shown on the PC.
4. Keep the desktop app open. Every scan and passport photo now goes to the PC with one tap.

Scan to PC, send files to PC, or receive files from the PC — a local Wi‑Fi file transfer between your phone and your desktop. Files stay on your network and are not uploaded to a SendToDesk cloud. Other apps can share any file to the same PC through the Android Share menu.


WHY SHOPS AND OFFICES SWITCH

• One app for passport size photos, document scanning and PDF compression
• The file is already on the computer — no downloading from chat
• Customer photo to printed passport photo in under a minute
• History on the phone so any scan or photo can be sent again
• Works on the shop Wi‑Fi; guest networks and VPNs may block pairing


PRIVACY

Scanning, background removal and compression run on the device. Transfers stay on your local network. Pair only with a computer you recognize. Ads may appear in the Android app; they are separate from scanning and sending.


SendToDesk — passport size photo maker, camera scanner to PDF, PDF compressor in KB, and one‑tap send to PC.
```
